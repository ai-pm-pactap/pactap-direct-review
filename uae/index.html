      Good questions. Clear answers.

      What does DDP pricing include?+

      Your product cost, shipping and applicable taxes and duties, together for your delivery destination. Your written quote will need to set out what’s included, any exclusions and how long the price is valid.

      Need help choosing your packaging?+

      Choose “Help me choose” and tell us what you’ll pack or carry. Add a quantity and destination; the specification can be reviewed from there. If your measurements are in inches, include them in your message with the units.

      What about quantity and delivery?+

      Tell us the quantity and timing you need. Minimum quantities and production, dispatch and delivery dates depend on your product and destination. We’ll confirm them with your written quote—not by submitting this form. Terms and Conditions apply.

      Can I request samples?+

      Yes—tell us what samples, artwork proofs or documents you need, including any food-contact or certification requirements. Availability, charges and supporting evidence need to be confirmed for your order. The 3D views are illustrations, not manufacturing specifications or certifications.
